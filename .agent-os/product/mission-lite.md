# Product Mission (Lite)

CattleNutrition Pro is a collaboration platform that connects cattle nutrition and veterinary consultants with cattle producers for professional livestock management services by providing comprehensive operational management tools and expert consultation workflows.

CattleNutrition Pro serves cattle producers and feedlot operators who need expert nutritional and veterinary guidance, along with nutrition and veterinary consultants who provide professional services to multiple operations. Unlike generic farm management software, we provide a specialized two-sided marketplace with real-time compliance monitoring and industry-specific workflows designed exclusively for cattle operations.
